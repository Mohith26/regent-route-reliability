Route study

Honolulu to Maui, every 2025 departure

Each departure from 06:00 to 19:00 HST is planned leg by leg (route, and wing, foil or hull mode) and then flown against what the sea actually did, using NOAA buoy observations along both routes.

Limits are significant wave height. Defaults: take-off in sea state 3 (1.25 m), foil ~5 ft and hull ~3 ft as REGENT has described publicly; the 3.0 m cruise limit is my assumption. Range 156 nm at 156 kn, 20% reserve.
